# Guide artwork and photographs

The assembly photographs document Robbi and its components; no robot-camera captures or personal portraits are included. Banners and guide diagrams are first-party project assets.

Product reference images under `parts/` illustrate the selected components. Their supplier/product sources are linked in [the parts list](../01-parts.md) and [BOM](../bom.csv). Supplier images and trademarks retain their respective rights and are excluded from claims of first-party artwork ownership.
