A SMALL GUIDE

Choreograph a moment.

Place waypoints on the floor, then drag their arrows to decide where Robbi looks. Each point describes the movement into that point. Duplicate a point to turn in place. Choose Media & speech under On arrival to add faces, screen images, videos, sounds, or speech.

Right-drag to orbit, middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hold Shift for 1 cm / 1° adjustments. In Heading mode, click anywhere on the floor to face it. Use Turn & drive for sharp corners.

Smooth curves respect body heading, wheel speed, acceleration, and minimum turning radius. Every waypoint is a full stop. Pauses and direction changes stay explicit, and the preview never moves the physical robot.
